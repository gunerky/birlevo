Privacy Policy

Birlevo Android app · Updated 18 October 2026

Which build you use

Previously distributed advertising-free builds, including the signed 0.1.11 package prepared before this update, do not use an advertising or analytics SDK. Advertising is being prepared for a later release. The advertising section below applies when those services are enabled, including development builds whose advertising test controls you choose to use. Updating this policy does not turn on advertising in an installed advertising-free build.

How your files are processed

Birlevo processes the images, videos, audio, documents and presentation projects you select on your Android device. No Birlevo account is required. Your selected files, document contents and project images are not uploaded to a Birlevo server or supplied as advertising targeting data.

Access and storage on your device

You choose files through Android’s system file picker. Processing may keep source copies, settings, project assets and results in private app storage so that supported tools can restore saved work. Completed background results may also be reopened. Older drafts can refer to an original file; moving or deleting that file may make it unavailable.

Clearing saved work or deleting a project removes its saved record and may remove private copies that can safely be identified as unused. Active work, retained results and shared project assets are protected from routine cleanup. Temporary copies used to open or share a result may be removed by Android or by the app when they are no longer needed. Use Save to keep a permanent exported copy.

Exporting and deleting files

You choose where to save an exported file. If you select a cloud storage provider or another sharing app, that service’s privacy practices apply to the file it receives. You can remove Birlevo’s local data in Android settings or uninstall the app. Your original files and files exported to a location you chose remain there and must be deleted separately.

Advertising and privacy choices

Advertising-enabled builds use Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). When these services are activated, Google may collect and share with its advertising partners IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device/account identifiers such as advertising ID or app set ID, app/ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Google describes transmission protected by TLS. See Google’s SDK data disclosure and privacy policy.

Advertising requests follow the provider’s current eligibility and applicable regional privacy choices. Where required, a privacy message is shown and an Ad privacy option lets you revisit your choices. Declining personalization does not necessarily remove all advertisements; limited ads may still be available depending on your choices and provider support. File tools remain available when the privacy check fails or advertising is unavailable.

Development builds with the Home advertising preview can contact Google after you save an eligible age choice; other development builds contact Google when you start the advertising privacy check or test banner. A test advertisement is not a promise that no network data is processed. You can manage or delete your Android advertising ID in system settings and review Google’s advertising controls. Removing Birlevo’s local data does not itself request deletion of data already held by Google; Google provides its own privacy controls.

Birlevo does not add a separate developer analytics service or upload your media for ad targeting. Advertising diagnostics handled by Google are separate from your local file processing.

Age group and advertising protections

In builds with the age-aware Home flow, you may choose an age group without giving your birth date. The group is saved privately on this device and can be changed through Ad privacy; clearing app data removes it. It is not sent as a custom ad-targeting field. Google receives the derived age-restriction settings needed to apply advertising protections. For 13–17 users, Birlevo disables personalized advertising and applies its conservative under-age protection and G content limit. Users under 13, those who do not give an age group, and users whose choice cannot be saved receive no new application ad requests. All file tools remain available. Adult users receive applicable regional privacy choices. A previous SDK initialization or previously transmitted data cannot be undone by changing the local group. The app is intended for the declared 13+ audience; this does not invite under-13 use.

This website

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process connection and visit information to deliver and protect the site and, where enabled, measure visits. Website services do not access media files stored inside the Android app. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Contact

For questions, write to support@birlevo.com. For privacy requests, write to privacy@birlevo.com. If you email us, your message and the contact details you provide are used to respond. Messages are forwarded to the developer’s inbox through Cloudflare Email Routing. Only send files you choose to provide for a specific support request.

Changes

This page will be updated when relevant services or data handling change. Advertising-enabled releases also require matching Google Play disclosures. The date above identifies this policy update; it does not identify the version installed on your device.
