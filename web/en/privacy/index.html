Privacy Policy

Birlevo Android app · Versions 0.1.0–0.1.7 · Effective 5 October 2026

How your files are processed

Birlevo processes the images, videos and presentation drafts you select on your Android device. This version has no account, cloud sync, advertising SDK or analytics SDK. Your selected files and exported results are not sent to a Birlevo server.

Access and storage on your device

The app accesses only the files you choose through Android's system file picker. Image and video processing may keep temporary copies and outputs in the app's private storage; files older than 24 hours are cleared when the tool is reopened. When creating a PDF from photos, selected images are temporarily copied into private app storage. This selection survives screen rotation, but must be selected again after the app process restarts. Save the PDF result before leaving the tool. These processing files are cleared after 24 hours when the tool is reopened. Presentation drafts are stored on your device. From version 0.1.6, newly added slide images are copied into private project storage so they remain available if the source is deleted or the app's cache is cleared. Earlier drafts may still reference the source file; those images can become unavailable if the source is moved or deleted. Project images are retained with the app's local data.

Opening or sharing a generated output creates a temporary copy for another app to read. These copies may be removed by Android when it clears the cache; copies older than seven days are also removed when another output is prepared. Use Save to keep a permanent exported copy in a location you choose.

Exporting and deleting files

You choose where to save an exported file in Android's system save screen. If you choose a cloud storage provider or another sharing app, that service's privacy practices apply to the file it receives. You can delete the app's local data in your device settings. Files you exported remain in the location you chose and must be deleted separately.

Data sharing

Using the app does not transfer personal data to the developer or advertising networks, and the app does not track your behavior.

This website

This page is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare's web analytics processes technical data about visits to the page; it does not access media files in the Android app.

Contact

For questions, write to support@birlevo.com. For privacy requests, write to privacy@birlevo.com. If you email us, your message and the contact details you provide are used to respond to your request. Messages to these addresses are forwarded to the developer's inbox through Cloudflare Email Routing.

Changes

If advertising, analytics, cloud sync or new file-processing services are added to the app, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before the relevant version is released.
