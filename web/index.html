Birlevo

Make working with files easier.

Birlevo’s Android closed test version 0.1.9 brings together practical tools for images, videos, audio and documents. Processing happens on your device; no account is required.

Images and videos

Convert and compress images. Trim a single video, choose the quality and resolution, and preview the actual result before saving.

Audio and documents

Extract audio from videos and create WAV or M4A files. Open PDFs and organize their pages, or read text and tabular files.

Create PDFs

Arrange your photos into a PDF. Create presentations with headings, text and images, and return to your draft later.

Available video codecs depend on your device. Conversion or editing may not produce a smaller file; equal or larger results require your confirmation to keep a separate copy. Your original files stay unchanged.
